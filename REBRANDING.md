# Adapt SmiProg for your company

**You may use, change and distribute SmiProg under your own brand, including commercially.**
Your changes may remain private; contributing improvements back is optional.

## What can I change?

- Company name, logo, colours and contact information.
- Product and catalogue links.
- Reference prices and interface text.
- Tool data, calculations and CNC output, with appropriate engineering review and testing.

A visible SmiCut name or link is not required in your version.

## How do I start?

1. [Download SmiProg](README.md#download-smiprog) and keep an unchanged backup.
2. List your desired changes and provide your logo, colours and product links.
3. Give the file and requirements to a web developer, or edit it yourself if you
   know HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no built-in branding editor.
4. Test your version on a computer and phone. Verify changed cutting data and CNC
   output for the intended machines before use.
5. Share the file or [publish it on your website](docs/getting-started.md#use-smiprog-on-your-website).

For ongoing customization, developers should use the readable source files and
rebuild the complete HTML. See [the development guide](docs/development.md).
For price changes, the source contains an editable price table and an import command.

## What must I keep?

Keep SmiCut AB's copyright notice, the full [MIT license](LICENSE) and the included
third-party icon notices. These are embedded in a comment near the beginning of
the complete HTML file. Leave them in place; they are not displayed in the app.
For multi-file distributions, include `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES`.

Describe modifications as your own version and do not imply SmiCut endorsement.

## Can I use the AI Material Selector?

Access to SmiCut's hosted AI service is separate from the software license.
[Request approval for your website](MATERIAL_SELECTOR.md). No API key needs to be
added after approval. The app can be used with manual material selection without AI.
